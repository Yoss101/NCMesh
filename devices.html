Batteries

Counterfeit Lithium Cell Alert

Avoid third-party marketplace vendors when sourcing standard 18650 or 21700 lithium cells. These generic units frequently advertise false capacity ratings, lack essential internal thermal protection, and present severe fire or ignition risks. To protect your hardware, purchase authenticated cells exclusively through certified suppliers like 18650 Battery Store, IMR Batteries, or Rokland.

Battery Management Systems

Compatibility Warning

Please ensure that the Battery Management System you intend to purchase matches the type of battery chemistry you are going to use!

Failure to use the same type as the batteries you are using will cause:

  • Hardware Failure
  • Dangerous Thermal Runaway or Fire Hazards
  • Severe Battery Cell Degradation and Permanent Capacity Loss
  • Inaccurate State-of-Charge (SoC) and Voltage Readings
  • Premature Overvoltage or Undervoltage Cutoffs

Solar Panels

Compatibility Warning

Please ensure that your solar panel's open-circuit voltage (Voc) is safely within the operating specifications of your BMS!

Failure to do so can cause:

  • Permanent BMS hardware failure
  • Catastrophic overcharging, leading to thermal runaway or fire hazards
  • Severe battery cell degradation and permanent capacity loss
  • Loss of critical overvoltage protection mechanisms
