MeshCore Next-Gen Routing Protocol

A smart routing mesh built to handle point-to-point text messaging efficiently, significantly reducing network traffic congestion.

Protocol Architecture Analysis

64-Hop Scale Capacity

Unlike standard mesh systems restricted to a 3-to-5 hop threshold to avoid airwave clutter, MeshCore securely scales up to an incredible 64 Hops. Because the directed-path protocol only wakes up one specific repeater at a time along the chain, messages can crawl across the entire state through small, low-altitude residential relays without generating data collisions.

Dynamic Directed-Paths

MeshCore mapping logic isolates routes to direct targets. A 64-byte payload overhead is physically reserved in the packet layer structure to explicitly map and follow a static route. Only assigned infrastructure nodes repeat the packet—leaving neighboring handheld units completely silent.

Stealthy Adverts Matrix

Instead of continuously broadcasting presence pings, MeshCore nodes protect channel availability by utilizing quiet, fixed intervals for Adverts (Node Announcements). Running natively on a multi-hour basis up to a 24-hour cycle, it maps topology alterations without generating packet storms.

Operational Grid Impact

By preventing client devices from wasting energy on repeating background data traffic, MeshCore client nodes only transmit when you explicitly execute a command. This keeps pocket gear alive longer in the field, while focusing critical solar node power reserves purely on high-utility infrastructure paths.

Types of MeshCore Nodes

Handheld & Companion Nodes

Your everyday portable radio paired to your smartphone via Bluetooth. Used for sending texts and managing app settings while staying silent when idle to save phone and device battery.

Dedicated Neighborhood Repeaters

Unattended radios placed high on rooftops, attics, or poles. Running dedicated repeater firmware, their only job is to calculate direct routes and quickly relay messages through your local community.

Long-Range Backbone Nodes

High-elevation or higher-power setups positioned on mountain peaks and towers. These strategic hub nodes handle heavy network traffic and bridge long-distance signal gaps across county lines.

Solar-Powered Outdoor Nodes

Weatherproof outdoor enclosures equipped with Solar Panels and batteries. Designed to run completely off-grid 24/7 to keep the network alive even during bad weather or power outages.
