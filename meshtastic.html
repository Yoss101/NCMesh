How Meshtastic Works

No Cell Service Needed

Meshtastic radios communicate directly device-to-device using low-power radio waves. Stay connected during cell network outages, natural disasters, or while traveling through remote backcountry trails.

Community-Powered Range

Every radio automatically acts as a repeater (unless using Client Mute), hopping messages from node to node over mountains and across town. The more people that join in your area, the stronger and wider your network becomes.

Public & Private Channels

Join open local channels like LongFast to talk with nearby radio enthusiasts, or set up secure, end-to-end encrypted private channels just for your family, group, or emergency team.

Off-Grid GPS & Mapping

Pair your radio to your iOS or Android phone using Bluetooth. Use the app to view real-time location updates from group members on offline maps without needing cellular data.

Types of Meshtastic Radios

Handheld & Pocket Nodes

Compact everyday radios—like the LILYGO T-Echo or SenseCAP T1000-E—that pair to your phone via Bluetooth. Carry them in your pocket or clip them to a backpack to text and share live GPS maps on the move.

Home Base Stations

Radios like the Station G3, Heltec v4 or a RAK Wisblock with RAK4631 placed in an upstairs window, attic, or roof ridge. Kept in the default Client mode, these home nodes act as indoor hubs to help bridge signal coverage across your local neighborhood.

Hilltop & High-Rise Repeaters

Dedicated nodes mounted high up on radio towers, mountaintops, or tall buildings. Configured in specialized Router modes, these long-range hubs relay signals over long distances to connect entire counties.

Solar-Powered Outdoor Nodes

Weatherproof enclosures combining ultra-low-power boards (like RAK WisBlock) with Solar Panels and batteries. These units run 24/7 completely independent of the power grid to keep the mesh alive during storms.
