Meshtastic

Deploy a pop-up, decentralized communication network anywhere you go. Perfectly suited for backcountry camping trips, group hikes, or crowded festival gatherings, it lets your group spin up ad-hoc text messaging and precise GPS location sharing on the fly—completely independent of cell towers, Wi-Fi infrastructure, or monthly subscription fees.

How Meshtastic Works

No Cell Service Needed

Meshtastic radios communicate directly device-to-device using low-power radio waves. Stay connected during cell network outages, natural disasters, or while traveling through remote backcountry trails.

Community-Powered Range

Every radio automatically acts as a repeater (unless using Client Mute), hopping messages from node to node over mountains and across town. The more people that join in your area, the stronger and wider your network becomes.

Public & Private Channels

Join open local channels like LongFast to talk with nearby radio enthusiasts, or set up secure, end-to-end encrypted private channels just for your family, group, or emergency team.

Off-Grid GPS & Mapping

Pair your radio to your iOS or Android phone using Bluetooth. Use the app to view real-time location updates from group members on offline maps without needing cellular data.

Meshtastic Device Roles

Client

The standard role for everyday portable radios—like the LILYGO T-Echo or SeeedStudio T1000-E. It sends, receives, and intelligently repeats messages, utilizing smart delays to prevent network packet collisions while helping to expand the mesh on the move.

Client Mute

A specialized role that can send and receive its own messages but never forwards or repeats packets from other devices. It is ideal for secondary pocket devices or highly congested areas to drastically reduce network noise and airtime load.

Client Base

A personal base station role designed for well-positioned attic or rooftop home nodes. It functions like a standard client but prioritizes rebroadcasting messages to and from your favorited nodes, bridging the gap for weaker indoor handhelds.

Router

Dedicated backbone infrastructure nodes mounted on mountaintops, towers, or strategic peaks. They aggressively prioritize forwarding all network traffic while completely disabling screens and Bluetooth to optimize power efficiency and mesh range.
